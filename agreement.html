Inspection agreement (sample)

Sample text written for this demo. It is not from 5starinspect.com and has not been reviewed by 5 Star Building Inspections or a lawyer. 5 Star's real inspection agreement will replace it.

This is the agreement you accept when you book. It covers what the inspection is and what it is not.

1. Scope

The inspection is a visual, non-invasive look at the home's readily accessible systems and components on the day of the inspection, done under Florida Statutes Chapter 468, Part XV, and the Florida home inspection standards of practice. Inspectors do not move furniture, open walls, or test items that are shut off or unsafe to operate.

2. Not a guarantee

The report describes the condition seen at the time of the inspection. It is not a warranty, an insurance policy, or a code-compliance inspection. Any limited warranty offered is covered by its own terms.

3. Report

The report is for the client named in the booking. It is released after payment is received and this agreement is accepted. If you ask us to, we will also send it to your realtor.

4. Payment and cancellation

No payment is taken when you book online. You pay when you receive your report or at the inspection. Please give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancelling within 24 hours of the inspection carries a $75 charge.

5. Access and safety

You agree to arrange access to the property, with utilities on, and to tell us about known hazards, pets, or alarms.

6. Disputes

Please tell us about any concern within 10 days of finding it and before making repairs, so we can look at it again.
