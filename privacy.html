Privacy Policy (sample)

Sample text written for this demo. It is not from 5starinspect.com and has not been reviewed by 5 Star Building Inspections or a lawyer. 5 Star's real privacy policy will replace it.

What we collect

When you book, we collect your name, phone, email, the property address and details, optional realtor and access information, and the services you choose.

How we use it

We use it to schedule and perform your inspection, send your report, and contact you about your booking. If you opt in, we send text messages about your inspection. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing

We share your report only with you and the people you ask us to send it to, such as your realtor. We use a scheduling service (Cal.com) to manage bookings. We do not sell your information, and we do not share your phone number or text consent with third parties for their marketing.
