Using this site

This site lets you request and book a home inspection. Prices shown are sample prices and are confirmed before your inspection.

Bookings

A booking holds a 2-hour arrival window. We may contact you to adjust the time if needed. Your inspection is also covered by the inspection agreement.

Text messages

If you opt in, you agree to receive automated texts about your inspection, such as confirmations, reminders, schedule changes, and report-ready notices. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of booking.
